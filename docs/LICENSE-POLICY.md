# Quantum Control License Policy

Quantum Control uses the Starlight Unit Studios Quantum Control Community Source License 1.0.

## Commercial hosting is allowed

A company or individual may use Quantum Control to operate paid hosting, managed servers, applications, storage, backups, support, and other infrastructure services.

Customers may receive authenticated access that is limited to resources provided or managed for them. The required Quantum Control and Starlight Unit Studios attribution must remain visible.

## Product resale is not allowed

Quantum Control itself may not be sold, rented, sublicensed, white-labeled, or offered as a standalone paid control-panel, SaaS, or general Control API product without prior written permission.

This distinction allows the intended KeyHelp-replacement use case without allowing another party to rebrand and resell Quantum Control as its own panel.

## Distribution

A distributed modification must retain the required notices, identify itself as unofficial, provide corresponding source code, and remain under the same license.

## Third-party boundaries

Managed operating systems, packages, web servers, databases, container runtimes, and tools retain their own licenses. Detection or administration through Quantum Control does not relicense them.
