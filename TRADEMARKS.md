# Quantum Control Branding and Trademark Policy

## Protected project identity

The following names and visual elements identify the Starlight Unit Studios project:

- `Quantum Control`
- `Quantum Control by Starlight Unit Studios`
- `qcored`
- `Starlight Unit Studios`
- associated official logos, word marks, interface marks, and origin notices

No trademark registration is asserted by this file unless separately documented. Copyright, naming, unfair-competition, and other applicable rights remain reserved.

## Required presentation

The product name is **Quantum Control**. A prominent first reference should preferably use `Quantum Control by Starlight Unit Studios`.

Built-in names, copyright notices, About information, source attribution, and official origin notices must remain visible and must not be removed, replaced, covered, or presented in a misleadingly minor role.

An operator may add a clearly subordinate notice such as `Operated by Example Hosting`. The operator notice must not replace Quantum Control branding or imply that the operator is Starlight Unit Studios.

A publicly distributed modified version must state:

`This is an unofficial modification of Quantum Control and is not maintained or officially supported by Starlight Unit Studios.`

## Customer-facing hosting use

The Community Source License permits Quantum Control to be used in commercial hosting and managed-service environments, including limited customer access. Such access does not permit removal of Quantum Control branding or presentation of the software as an operator-owned white-label control panel.

## Technical identifiers

Compatibility identifiers such as `quantum-control`, `qcored`, `QUANTUM_CONTROL_*`, service names, package names, API paths, repository slugs, and domain components may remain unchanged. They are machine-facing identifiers and do not authorize another product to claim official Starlight Unit Studios status.

## Third-party names

Names such as Linux, systemd, KeyHelp, Apache, Nginx, PHP, MariaDB, PostgreSQL, Docker, Ollama, and other third-party products are used only to describe compatibility, detection, or administration. Their marks and names remain subject to the rights of their respective owners.

## Permissions

Any branding change outside the terms of the Quantum Control Community Source License requires prior express written permission from the rights holder. Permission for one deployment does not create a general permission for another deployment.
